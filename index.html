Welcome to the World of Light!

Light is a form of energy that travels in waves and allows us to see the world around us. These waves come in different sizes and frequencies, creating the electromagnetic spectrum. The light visible to our eyes is called visible light and includes all the colors of the rainbow. However, visible light makes up only a small portion of the entire electromagnetic spectrum. Explore this website to learn about the different types of electromagnetic waves and how they work!

About Light

The electromagnetic spectrum includes many types of waves that we use in science, technology, and everyday life. These include radio waves, microwaves, infrared, ultraviolet, X-rays, and gamma rays.
